# Offer Structure

> **Status:** PROPOSED · **Source:** Playbook v1.0 §4.6 · **Updated:** 2026-11-09
>
> **Related:** [Pricing & packages](../03-commercial/pricing-and-packages.md) · [Customer relationships](../02-go-to-market/customer-relationships.md) · [Templates](../templates/)

The customer journey is framed in four service phases:

| # | Phase | What it is | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free AI Opportunity Preview** | Public-information-based analysis, one or two (sometimes up to three) tailored agent ideas, and a concise business rationale. A lead-generation investment, **not** a free engineering project. | [AI Opportunity Preview](../templates/ai-opportunity-preview.md) |
| 2 | **Paid AI Discovery** | Customer interviews, process mapping, API/data feasibility, scope, KPI baselines, architecture, and risk assessment. | [Discovery Report](../templates/discovery-report.md) |
| 3 | **Paid AI Pilot and Production Implementation** | Demonstrate a limited proof of value and then integrate an approved, tested production agent into the application. | [Pilot Acceptance Report](../templates/pilot-acceptance-report.md) |
| 4 | **Optional AI Agent Operations** | Monitor performance, support the feature, optimize it, and periodically identify new opportunities. | [Monthly Operations Report](../templates/monthly-operations-report.md) |

The commercial price list distinguishes **Discovery**, **Pilot**, **Production**, and **Managed Support** as separate deliverables (see [pricing & packages](../03-commercial/pricing-and-packages.md)). A phased proposal must be clear about what work is included in each stage and must **not double-bill** pilot code or analysis that is reused later.
