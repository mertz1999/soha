# External Resources

> **Status:** Reference · **Source:** Playbook v1.0 Appendix C, §5.3, §8.5 · **Updated:** 2026-11-09

These links were mentioned as possible research resources. Their content, accuracy, pricing, and current accessibility should be checked when used.

## Lead research (Iran)

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| [Tehran Index company directory](https://tehranindex.com/companies) | Iranian technology-company discovery |
| [Cafe Bazaar](https://cafebazaar.net/) | Iranian Android apps, product categories, developers |
| [Myket](https://myket.ir/) | Another Iranian Android-app discovery source |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Company profiles and decision-maker identification |

Plus: official company websites, feature pages, app documentation, help centers, and public product demonstrations.

## AI risk and security standards

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
