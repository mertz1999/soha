## What each decision means

1. **Business model — Option B: AI Innovation + Implementation**, rather than idea-only consulting or a pure self-serve agent marketplace. Option C (packaged agent platform) remains a *potential future path* — see [reuse & productization](../04-operations/reuse-and-productization.md).
2. **Targeting** — Test **multiple software categories initially** instead of committing prematurely to one vertical. Evaluate which segment converts best. See [segment validation experiment](../02-go-to-market/segment-validation-experiment.md).
3. **Customer acquisition** — **Founder-led, highly personalized sales**, with AI assisting research and proposal preparation. Do not launch with unreviewed bulk automated outreach.
4. **Customer relationship** — A collaborative, trust-based engagement designed to evolve from discovery and implementation to an ongoing AI innovation partnership.
5. **Monetization** — **Hybrid model:** paid discovery and implementation plus optional recurring support and optimization.
6. **Pricing position** — **Balanced specialist**: serious engineering capability for established software companies, with paid pilots, production delivery, and optional monthly services.
