# Iran-Specific Engineering & Compliance Considerations

> **Status:** TO VALIDATE · **Source:** Playbook v1.0 §8.6 · **Updated:** 2026-11-09
>
> **Related:** [Delivery quality checklist](delivery-quality-checklist.md) · [Key partners](../05-organization/key-partners.md) · [Risk register](../06-execution/risk-register.md)

Before launch and before individual deployments, ENSA should validate:

| Area | What to validate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| AI providers | Availability, pricing, and contractual permission for local and foreign AI model providers. | TO VALIDATE |
| Hosting & data residency | Hosting architecture; whether data can/should remain within Iranian infrastructure. | TO VALIDATE |
| Persian language | Persian-language evaluation quality, domain vocabulary, right-to-left UI, and document formats. | TO VALIDATE |
| Connectivity | Connectivity reliability, failover, and degraded-mode behavior. | TO VALIDATE |
| Sector rules | Industry-specific rules (especially healthcare, fintech, insurance, and other regulated use cases). | TO VALIDATE |
| Legal & tax | Applicable privacy, security, service, tax, corporate, invoicing, and cross-border rules in Iran, reviewed by qualified local advisors. | TO VALIDATE |
| Human approvals | Responsible human approvals for actions that alter money, records, accounts, contracts, or sensitive data. | Required by design |

**Note:** A regulatory claim about Iranian AI-as-a-service frameworks appeared earlier in the strategy discussion; the applicability and current legal requirements must be confirmed with local legal counsel. These documents do not purport to decide whether ENSA needs any particular license.

When an item is validated, record the source (advisor, regulation, vendor quote) and date next to it.
